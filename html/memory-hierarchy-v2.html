What changed in v2 — the relabeling that makes the metaphor honest:
· L1/L2 are the processor itself (weights = firmware), not caches — no data flows through them.
· L3 is the provider prefix-cache — the real cache, holding hot ICM lines; that's why 98.8% of tokens hit.
· the physical files are the DRIVE — the source; RAM pages from it; the cache keeps the hot lines resident next to the CPU.
· the processor can write to the drive directly (notes, distillation) — the angled path that bypasses RAM.
· two axes, one mechanism: latency (fast→slow) vs persistence (what survives). The cache is a copy, never the source.
